How to use the Content Creator Studio

  1. Paste your script into the analyzer.
  2. Check word count, character count, sentence count and estimated voice-over duration.
  3. Choose a target platform and adjust speaking speed if needed.
  4. Clean AI-generated formatting with one click.
  5. Save your script as a local draft.
  6. Send the script to the vertical teleprompter or type directly inside the teleprompter editor.
  7. Choose speed multiplier or target duration mode.
  8. Start the countdown and record your video naturally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this teleprompter free?

Yes. The Pratix.io Content Creator Studio is free to use in your browser.

Does it upload my script?

No. Script analysis, text cleaning, draft storage and teleprompter playback run locally in your browser.

Can I use it for TikTok and Reels?

Yes. The vertical 9:16 teleprompter is designed for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Can I save drafts?

Yes. Drafts are stored in localStorage on your own device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.
