FAQ

      
Frequently asked questions

      

        
What is an llms.txt file?
llms.txt is a proposed standard file at your domain root that gives AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude a concise, markdown-formatted map of your best content.

        
Is this llms.txt generator free?
Yes. Every feature, including copy and download, is free and unlimited — there is no sign-up and your input never leaves your browser.

        
Where should I upload the llms.txt file?
Upload it to the root of your domain so it resolves directly at /llms.txt, next to your robots.txt and sitemap.xml.

        
Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. Generation runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript; nothing you type is sent to any server.

        
Does llms.txt replace a sitemap?
No. A sitemap lists every crawlable URL for search engines, while llms.txt curates your most valuable pages specifically for AI engines.
