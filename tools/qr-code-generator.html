FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. Every feature, including PNG and SVG downloads, is free and unlimited — there is no sign-up and no watermark.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. A QR code is simply an encoded image of your content, so it keeps working forever and never expires.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Generation runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript; your content never leaves your device.

PNG or SVG — which should I choose?

Use PNG for websites, presentations and screens; use SVG for print materials because it stays sharp at any size.

Can I use the QR codes commercially?

Yes. QR codes you create are yours to use in marketing, packaging, events or products with no attribution required.
