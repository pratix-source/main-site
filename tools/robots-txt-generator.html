FAQ

      
Frequently asked questions

      

        
What is a robots.txt file?
A robots.txt file is a plain text file at your domain root that tells crawlers which parts of your site they may access. AI bots and search engines read it before fetching your pages.

        
How do I block AI bots from training on my content?
Add Disallow: / rules for AI training crawlers such as GPTBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Bytespider and Applebot-Extended. This generator writes those rules for you in one click.

        
Does blocking AI training bots affect my Google rankings?
No. Search crawlers such as Googlebot and Bingbot are separate from AI training crawlers. Blocking GPTBot or CCBot does not hurt your search rankings.

        
Can I allow AI search but block AI training?
Yes. Bots like OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot power AI answer engines that cite your pages, while GPTBot and CCBot collect training data. You can set different rules for each group.

        
Where do I upload the robots.txt file?
Upload it to the root of your domain so it resolves directly at /robots.txt, next to your sitemap.xml and llms.txt files.
