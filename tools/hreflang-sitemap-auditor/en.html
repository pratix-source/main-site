    Frequently asked questions

    Does the auditor upload my sitemap?

    No. Parsing happens locally in your browser. The tool does not send your sitemap or HTML to a server.

    What does it check?

    It checks hreflang reciprocity, x-default, canonical URLs, duplicate sitemap URLs, absolute URLs and language coverage.

    Can it replace Google Search Console?

    No. It is a fast pre-publication check. Search engines may apply additional rules and rendering checks.
